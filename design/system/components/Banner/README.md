A full-width message across the top of the board, below the top bar: connection trouble, a failed save, the meeting having ended.

- Three tones, `info`, `warning` and `error`, shown by the colour of its thick left edge. The words always say what is wrong, so colour is never the only signal.
- One banner at a time. When several apply, the screen shows the highest priority (docs/product/mvp-screens.md, Connection states and banners).
- Full sentences. Engine refusal messages keep their own wording. An action, where there is one, is a small secondary button: "Retry", "Reload", "Back up", "Dismiss", "Copy as text".
