The facilitator's next step, always in view.

- One `primary` button for the next step in the flow ("Start discussion", "Discuss #4", "Finish topic", "Decide", "Copy as text"), then `secondary` buttons for the rest of that state.
- Phones: stuck to the bottom of the screen, full width, holding the primary on the right and at most one other button, so it fits at 320px. A state with two or more other actions shows "More" instead, which opens a sheet with them. It hides while the topic field has focus, so the keyboard has room.
- Wide screens: a stacked block at the top of the side panel, every button shown, with the Facilitator controls in place below it.
- The states and their buttons are in docs/product/mvp-screens.md, The facilitator action bar.
