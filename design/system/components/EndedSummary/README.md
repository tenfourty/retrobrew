The final board once a meeting has ended, at every width: one list in two parts rather than columns.

- "Discussed": the finished topics in the order they were finished, numbered 1, 2, 3 in tabular numerals. "None" if nothing was discussed.
- "Not discussed": every other topic, most votes first. Left out when it would be empty.
- Each topic is a `TopicCard` showing its votes and no actions.
- The two headings borrow Party's display face in both modes: the end of a meeting is the one Party moment Business allows.
- The summary is a region named "Final board". The lists are a numbered `ol` for "Discussed" and a plain `ul` for "Not discussed".
